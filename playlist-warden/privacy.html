Privacy Policy

Playlist Warden for YouTube · Last updated: 2026-08-05

TL;DR

Playlist Warden stores everything locally in your browser profile. It has no backend, no analytics, no tracking, and shares nothing with anyone. The only network requests it makes go to Google's own servers (googleapis.com / youtube.com) as part of the features you trigger.

Who we are

Playlist Warden for YouTube („the extension“) is developed and published by martjn (martjn.net). Contact: via the project's source repository listed on the app page.

What data the extension accesses

  • YouTube playlist data (video IDs, titles, channels, durations, availability, region/age/category metadata) — read via the official YouTube Data API v3, and only for playlists you act on.
  • Contributor avatars on collaborative playlists — read from the page you are already viewing (YouTube shows „who added what“ only as avatar images); the stable photo ID is used as the contributor key.
  • Your Google OAuth access token — created when you sign in with Google; used to read and (on playlists you own) modify playlist contents on your behalf. Tokens are short-lived and renew through your existing Google session.
  • Your settings — per-playlist caps/rules, audit log, job history.

Where data is stored

All of the above is stored exclusively in your browser profile (browser.storage). Nothing is transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by the developer. There is no developer backend at all.

Sharing and third parties

  • No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • The only external communication is with Google (YouTube Data API and Google sign-in), initiated by your explicit actions.

Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure

The extension's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: information is used solely to provide the extension's user-facing features, is never used for advertising, is never read by humans except with your affirmative direction, and is never transferred except to provide those features.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data lives in your browser profile, you control it completely. Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data. Signing out or revoking the extension's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions invalidates its access tokens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new „last updated“ date.
